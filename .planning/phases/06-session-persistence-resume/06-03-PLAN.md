## Planner Assumptions (PA-n)

- **PA-2 (RESEARCH Open Question 2):** ResumeCard is a SIBLING component, not a SessionBanner extension — the Phase-5-tested banner stays untouched; shared presentation rides on byte-identical `.session-banner*` classes (D-07). Zero new CSS, zero new i18n keys (RESEARCH A5 verbatim-copy reuse accepted).
- **PA-3 (RESEARCH Open Question 3):** a paused-at-kill record resumes PLAYING from the frozen position, not paused — cinema context: relaunch intent is to continue watching, and pause is one tap away. UI-SPEC "partial" row covers the display contract.
- **PA-5 (RESEARCH Open Question 5):** on a successful resume tap, `updateSettings({ offsetMs: record.offsetMs })` pushes the record's offset into settings — four-way agreement (engine/session/banner/controls). The follow-on hook offset-effect write is one extra ~100-byte record write, semantically a no-op.
- **PA-6 (RESEARCH Open Question 6):** no `visibilitychange` re-read of the persisted record — out of FILE-03 scope for a v1 single-tab phone PWA (RESEARCH Pitfall 7 last-writer-wins accepted). Also no `navigator.storage.persist()` and no BroadcastChannel: the 6h expiry sits far below every storage-eviction horizon (RESEARCH Pitfall 6).
- **PA-7 (RESEARCH Pattern 4 / Pitfall 9 reaffirmed):** expiry is evaluated EXACTLY ONCE per app launch in the boot mount effect. A tap-time expiry re-check on the resume handler was considered and REJECTED: UI-SPEC I-2's chain contains no such step, research locks load-only evaluation, and the orphaned-card case (app backgrounded past the threshold, card tapped hours later) converges safely — restore lands past the last cue, `onEnded` fires, the view converges to selection, the record clears via the 06-02 persist guard. No mid-use expiry anywhere (see prohibitions).
- **Canon breadcrumb (prohibition recall §B canon-referral):** injection/XSS on record fields is canon — record fields render through React text nodes only (no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` anywhere in the project); covered by threat T-06-03-03 and `/gsd-secure-phase`; NOT minted as a prohibition.
